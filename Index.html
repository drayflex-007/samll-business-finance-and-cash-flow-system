THE PROBLEM

Your business can be profitable and still run out of cash.

  • You check your bank balance before agreeing to almost any purchase.
  • A large payment arrives and somehow the money disappears within days.
  • Your business looks profitable but paying bills still feels stressful.
  • You don't know what your cash position will look like next month.
  • You only look closely at your numbers when something goes wrong.

The answer isn't necessarily more complicated accounting. It's having a simple financial system you can actually use.

With the Finance System
  • Income and expenses are recorded in one place.
